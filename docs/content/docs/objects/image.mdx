A 2D plane in the scene. Choose `image` or `video` media type:

- **image** — pick from imported images.
- **video** — pick from imported videos. Playback rate is adjustable.

## Synced video

One video per project can be designated the **synced video** — its playhead drives the audio progress bar instead of an audio file. Useful when you want a music-video to drive the timeline.

## Reactivity

Like models, Image objects have:

- **Audio Scale** — extra scale multiplier from a binding.
- **Spin** — continuous rotation around `+x/-x/+y/-y/+z/-z`.
- **Opacity**.
